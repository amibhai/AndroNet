1 (Fig. 1) - Block diagram. A root probe selects between a VPN capture path on the left, which relays traffic through a bounded queue and knows the owning app, and a root capture path on the right, which sees inbound and link-layer traffic. Both feed a canonical packet record in the centre. Below it, protocol dissection feeds four detection layers, statistical, signature, rule engine and flow and app detectors, which feed alerts. Export and a bounded user interface stream also read from the record.
